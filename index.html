Common Questions

Everything you need to know

Which intro pack should I choose?+

If you'd like to try Pilates at a relaxed pace, 6 classes in 2 weeks for $59 is about three classes a week. If you can come more often and want to build a routine quickly, 21 days unlimited for $75 lets you book as many classes as you like.

I've never done Pilates before. Is this for me?+

Absolutely. Many of our members started as complete beginners. Our Clinical Style classes are designed for newcomers, those returning to exercise, or anyone with injuries. We meet you exactly where you are.

How do I use my Black Friday code?+

Each offer on this page has its own code. Copy it, tap the button to choose your pack or plan, and enter the code at checkout.

How long do the offers run?+

The intro packs, 15% off new memberships, the Member Referral Special and the Check-in Draw run from 1 to 30 November 2026. The 10% off class packs is for Black Friday week only, 23 to 29 November.

How does the Member Referral Special work?+

Bring a friend to class with you and their class is free. If they'd like to keep going, they can get 1 week of unlimited classes for $30.

How do I enter the Check-in Draw?+

Post a story from the studio, tag @4youpilates and complete your weekly classes every week in November. Do all three and you're in the draw for 15% off your next month or exclusive 4You gifts.
